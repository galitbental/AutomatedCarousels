## 1. Business positioning

| **Brand lines** | More than jewelry · Wear what matters · A more meaningful you · Unique, meaningful, personal, always yours · Custom jewelry for life's most special moments |

## 2. Voice and language

### 2.1 Speaker — two voices, rotated for variety
| Voice | Who "I" is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Giver voice** | The person giving the gift (grandma, mother of the bride, mother-in-law, aunt…) | *"I gave it to her in the getting-ready room, before anyone else came in."* |
| **Founder story** | The owner tells a short personal moment the reader recognizes herself in | *"I still remember the necklace my grandmother gave me. I was twelve."* |
| **Brand voice** | Be Wished speaking (third person / "we") | *"So the words are already written for you."* |

Rules:
- Each carousel uses one voice; consecutive carousels should not repeat the same voice.
- Founder stories never invent sensitive facts (loss of a parent, illness, family details) unless the owner confirmed them.

### 2.2 Style rules
| Rule | ✓ / ✗ |
|---|---|
| **Short.** People don't like long reading. | ✗ "They are the gifts that feel chosen, personal, and connected to a real relationship." ✓ "It feels chosen. It carries her name." |
| **Positive framing**, not negative. Soften certainty. | ✗ "She will not remember the toast." ✓ "She might not remember the toast. She will keep this." |
| Tone: luxurious, quality, emotional, unique, light and pleasant. | — |
| Core words: *quality, personalized, ready-to-gift, heartfelt, keepsake*. Supporting: *elevated, timeless, effortless, cherished, curated, intentional*. | — |
| A **symbolic anchor** links jewelry to emotion (like the poem cards). | see 2.3 |
| Never mention **price**. | ✗ "The gifts we remember are not always the most expensive." |
| No body/size wording. | ✗ "not just her size" (diet association) |
| No fabricated customer stories or testimonials. | ✗ "She still has it. Ten years later." |
| All topics are open (loss, illness, faith, Confirmation). Loss/illness → gentle tone, never celebratory; no medical claims. | ✓ "Not a get-well gift. A keep-going gift." |

### 2.3 Symbolic anchors per jewelry
| Jewelry | Anchor |
|---|---|
| Pearl | A pearl forms slowly, inside the shell, and no two are alike. *"A pearl takes years to become one of a kind. So does she."* |
| Interlocking circles | A bond that holds; no beginning and no end. *"Two circles that hold."* · *"Thankful our circles crossed."* |
| Engraved disc | *"Engraved, not printed. So it is still there on the low days."* |
| Satellite chain | Small moments spaced along one continuous line — like joining a family. |
| Heart locket | Keeps her people close (1–2 photos). *"Flowers fade. This one keeps her people close."* |
| Stars | *"Sisters are like stars — you don't always see them, but they're always there."* |
| Birthstone | Her month, her color — made only for her. |

## 3. Hooks

Alternate between two types:
- **[ק] Short dramatic statement** — *"You have one morning to say eighteen years."* · *"Jewelry is beautiful. Meaning makes it unforgettable."*
- **[ש] Direct question** — *"What do you give the woman who is about to become your daughter?"*

Approved hook patterns (owner's winners):
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Giver memory (past tense) | *"This made her cry… I gave it to my daughter just before the ceremony."* |
| Giver intention | *"I already loved her. I wanted a wedding day gift that will say it out loud."* |
| Quote | *"We both love the same man."* |
| Objection | *"For the woman who says 'I don't need anything.'"* |
| Buyer's struggle | *"When you want to say something meaningful, but don't know how."* |
| Symbolic | *"A pearl takes years to become one of a kind. So does she."* |
| Chosen-not-accepted | *"She was becoming family that day. I wanted the gift to say she was chosen, not accepted."* |

Rules: max 2 sentences · must work before the "…more" cut · always offer **3 options** for the owner to choose ·
never reuse or closely paraphrase a previously used hook.

## 4. Copy structure

**Post / carousel logic:** Hook → Problem (short and sharp) → Solution (product + poem card with her name and your
closing line + Sterling Silver or 14K Gold Filled + ready to gift) → CTA.

**CTA** — alternate direct-sales and emotional-inviting:
- Save this for your next gift idea
- Shop through the link in bio
- Which message would you choose?
- Make her feel like family. Link in bio
- Give her something she can hold on to
- Send love in a box

**Caption template** (from the 6 final captions):
```
<Hook line> 💛

<2–3 short paragraphs, ~55 characters per line:
 problem → what the gift is → the personalization>

<Product facts: Sterling Silver or 14K Gold Filled, ready to gift>

Save this for your next gift idea 🔖
Shop through the link in bio.

<hashtags>
```

**Emoji:** none on slides; at most 2 in the caption (💛 after the hook line, 🔖 next to "Save this").

**Hashtags:** English only · **3–30, a different count and set for every carousel** · mix broad / mid / niche
(recipient, giver, occasion, jewelry type, gift ideas) · always end with **#bewished #bewishedgifts #morethanjewelry** ·
never an identical set to a previous carousel.

**Slide count: 7 to 10.**

| Type | Example (published) | Structure |

| **Objection** | I Don't Need Anything | Quote cover → 3–4 black slides building the argument → emotional photo + CTA |
| **Checklist** | The Ready-to-Gift Checklist | Cover → ✓ question + short answer per slide → "That is a BeWished gift." + button |
| **Gift guide by recipient** | When you want to say something meaningful | Cover → one recipient per slide (eyebrow label "GRATITUDE + LOVE", title "For Mom", gold subline, grey line) → packaging CTA |

## 6. Visual system

### 6.1 Color palette
| Name | Hex | Character | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Royal Black | `#0A0A0A` | bold, sophisticated, timeless | slide backgrounds, panels |
| Velvet Charcoal | `#1C1A1A` | modern, elegant, versatile | secondary backgrounds |
| Antique Gold | `#C79A3B` | prestige, heritage | decorative lines, icons |
| Rich Brass | `#A97A1F` | warm, luxurious | shadows of gold, hover |
| Champagne Gold | `#E2C98A` | radiant, refined | accent words, CTA buttons, rules |
| Soft Ivory | `#F5EBDD` | pure, elevated | light text alternative |
| Warm Taupe | `#B8A089` | natural, understated | small secondary text |

### 6.2 Typography
- Headlines: **Playfair Display** bold, white, with Champagne Gold accent words.
- Secondary lines: small sans-serif, light grey or gold.
- Eyebrow labels: small, uppercase, letter-spaced ("GIFT GUIDE", "BEFORE YOU BUY", "MOTHER'S GIFT").
- Optional script overlay for a short phrase on a photo (e.g. "Forever connected").
- Quotes: italic, with a thin gold vertical bar.

### 6.3 Elements
- Format 1080×1350 (4:5).
- Short gold rule above the text block.
- CTA button: Champagne Gold fill, black uppercase text ("SHOP THROUGH THE LINK IN BIO", "SAVE THIS FOR YOUR NEXT GIFT IDEA").
- "›" swipe arrow on the right edge of every slide except the last.
- Checklist slides: gold circled check icon.

### 6.4 Layout rules (decided while building)
- Readable content at the bottom of the photo (poem card, box) → **black panel** below the photo.
- Dark, empty bottom → **text on the photo** with a soft gradient only.
- Text in a corner → soft diagonal gradient (≈145°) fading out by mid-frame.
- Never cover faces, jewelry or poem-card text.
- No gold text on golden bokeh — switch those words to white.
- Balanced line breaks; never leave one word alone on a line.
- Make text as large as possible without hiding the subjects.

### 6.5 Imagery
- Dark, warm, candlelit luxury: black/marble surfaces, gold bokeh, baby's breath, gold ribbon.
- Black Be Wished box with gold logo whenever the lid/closed box is shown; grey box OK when only open in hands.
- Giver + recipient scenes with the recipient wearing the jewelry; diverse models; real emotion.
- Generated images contain no text (text is added in design).
- When generating photos meant for text overlay, keep the lower third dark and free of key details.
- Don't reuse the same photo in carousels published close together.

## 7. Additional attributes

1. **Content pillars & rhythm** — rotate: brand-voice post → carousel → giver/founder story post → reel, then move to the next audience.
2. **Audience × occasion matrix** — track coverage (granddaughter, daughter-in-law, mother of the bride, mother of the groom, Confirmation, baptism, loss, warrior/survivor, mom, sister, best friend, wife; gaps: pregnancy, 18/21 birthdays, holidays).
3. **Accessibility** — sufficient text contrast on photos; alt text for every slide.
4. **Compliance** — only approved product facts; no price; no medical claims; no fabricated testimonials.
5. **Repetition guard** — track hooks, hashtag sets, photos, closing slides and speaker voice across posts.
6. **Success metrics** — prioritize saves and shares (the "Save this" CTA), then link clicks.
7. **Pre-publish checklist**
   - [ ] Hook ≤ 2 sentences, not used before
   - [ ] Speaker voice differs from the previous carousel
   - [ ] Symbolic anchor present
   - [ ] Only approved product facts; no price
   - [ ] 7–10 slides; swipe arrow on all but the last
   - [ ] No covered faces / jewelry / card text; no gold on gold bokeh; no orphan words
   - [ ] Caption follows the template; ≤ 2 emoji; none on slides
   - [ ] Hashtags 3–30, unique set, brand tags last
   - [ ] Images not reused from a recent carousel
